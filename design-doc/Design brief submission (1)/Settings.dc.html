When you drop an image

  1. 01Your browser reads the file into this tab’s memory.
  2. 02Every edit runs on a canvas, on your processor.
  3. 03The result is encoded here and saved to your Downloads.
  4. 04Close the tab and the images are gone from memory.

What does download

Only Konva’s own files: the app itself, and the background-removal model or HEIC decoder the first time you use them. Your images, file names and metadata are never part of a request.
